## About

I'm a Software Engineer at **Karooth IT (BD) Limited**, building secure, scalable enterprise platforms across the full stack — Angular micro-frontends on the client side, Spring Boot services on the backend, and the databases, messaging, and storage systems that hold them together.

My work centers on large-scale management information systems: beneficiary and payment operations for a social protection program, and institutional/training platforms used in production by real organizations. I care about clean architecture, secure-by-default APIs, and code that's still maintainable a year after it ships.

- 🎓 BSc in Computer Science & Engineering, University of Dhaka (2024)
- 📍 Based in Dhaka, Bangladesh
- 💼 Software Engineer at Karooth IT (BD) Limited since August 2024
